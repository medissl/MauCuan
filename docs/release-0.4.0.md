# MauCuan 0.4.0

The pet room now reserves separate, non-overlapping foreground areas for Miko, left furniture, right furniture and a toy. Wall illustrations occupy the upper band; the floor and rug sit behind the character. Head and face accessories remain aligned to the character artwork. The dialogue keeps its fixed space beneath the decorated interior. All 68 collectibles have a valid slot; malformed room values are discarded before rendering. Equipping another item replaces the value for that slot in an authenticated, owner-checked server operation. No free positioning or stacking is offered. Shop descriptions are friendlier and say which spot an item replaces.

Daily quizzes have five saved questions and one completion reward of five leaves, regardless of score. The first set retains the five app-introduction questions. Subsequent sets randomly prioritize unseen questions and exclude questions assigned in the previous seven Jakarta calendar days. There are 500 distinct prompts: five introduction questions, 300 everyday decision scenarios and 195 numerical practice variants, across 32 topic labels. These are authored scenario and arithmetic variants, not 500 different financial concepts. Completing 100 daily sets exposes the full bank before older material returns. Partial sessions resume after closing the app; unanswered questions still count as shown for repeat exclusion. Answer submissions are sequential and idempotent, with a server-side daily reward. Reopening or retrying cannot pay again. Offline failures show a retry action and preserve saved progress.

The bank is bundled in `src/lib/quiz-bank.json`; `scripts/generate-quiz.py` reproduces the content. The database migration carries the matching answer keys. Tests compare every client answer key with that migration. Examples teach everyday planning, borrowing costs, safety and simple whole-rupiah math, without recommending individual financial products. Investment-warning topics were cross-checked against [OJK's investment-warning FAQs](https://www.ojk.go.id/waspada-investasi/id/FAQ.aspx). The arithmetic scenarios are self-authored examples.

Economy: a daily check-in remains 10 leaves and 20 XP. Daily learning adds at most 5 leaves and no XP. Maximum daily leaves are therefore 15. New paid collectible prices are 1.5 times their previous cost, rounded up to a whole leaf; existing purchases retain historical cost. Free level gifts and all level gates remain unchanged. Level 120 still requires 1,190 check-ins (more than three years at one a day), and missed days do not remove progress. Quiz answers and pet gestures cannot accelerate levels. A regular quiz player has approximately the same purchasing pace as before because both maximum daily income and future prices increased by 50%; someone skipping quizzes earns cosmetics more slowly. This preserves level-gated long-term content without requiring spending money or creating transactions.

Miko's everyday dialogue now selects an expression by meaning: sleepy lines use rest, celebrations use happy, curiosity and money review use focused, playful mishaps use wink, and quiet companionship uses idle. Conversation no longer forces every line into the same wink. The chosen pet name remains a variable.

Transaction history offers a calendar and a monthly grouped list. The top-left month control uses a native month/year sheet. Calendar entries are orange, today is blue even when it contains entries, and past dates without entries are grey. Future dates are disabled. Tapping a date shows its entries beneath the calendar; the same income/expense filters apply in both views. The monthly list groups all matching entries by date and keeps existing edit actions. Grey means no transaction was recorded, not a failed check-in.

Validation: 55 automated tests, lint and TypeScript; Android and iOS production bundle exports. Tests cover 140 days of question selection, the first 100 days' full bank coverage, retry/resume/completion, native answer and month controls, leap calendars, date filtering, slot validation and disjoint foreground bounds at several phone widths. The hosted migration and rollback-only synthetic-account checks passed for daily rewards, invalid answers, ownership isolation, weekly exclusions, quiz-funded purchases and decoration replacement. A static review generated from the actual SVG components displays all collectible placements; it does not simulate native gestures. Physical device verification remains pending the new APK.

The existing Supabase security advisory about [leaked-password protection](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/password-security#password-strength-and-leaked-password-protection) remains; the new quiz tables introduce no database security advisory. Receipt parsing and approved mascot PNGs are unchanged.
