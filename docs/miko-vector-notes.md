# Miko vector concept

This is a separate code-authored SVG concept, not a production replacement or commissioned human illustration.

The same eye and ear geometry is reused on both sides with equal center offsets. Spot symbols are deliberately positioned; rosettes share one reusable shape. Markings stay separate from the contour, muzzle, eyes, and paws. Colors are flat and the artwork has no fur texture, generated shading, or raster image dependency.

The result is intentionally more graphic and simple than the previous illustrated concept. The SVG source is editable in Figma or a vector editor. A later character sheet should use these same components and proportions for additional expressions and poses.
